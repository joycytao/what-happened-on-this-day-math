# Product Reel Template

Four-week monthly Product Reel system for a verified worksheet package.

- Canvas: 1080×1920, 30 fps, approximately 10 seconds per Reel.
- Product cover and real worksheet evidence are primary.
- Four weekly hooks: package discovery, differentiated levels, reading/math connection, and monthly routine.
- Every Reel ends with the month-specific TPT or redirect CTA.
- Product claims must be source-asset-only; do not expose answer keys or invent outcomes.

Source contracts: `docs/product-reels-content-plan.md`, `docs/product-reel-workstream.md`, and `src/product-reel-workstream.mjs`.
