# Campaign Reminder Reel Template

Two-variant campaign reminder system shared by free worksheet and early-bird sale campaigns.

- Outputs two publication items per campaign: launch and ending-soon.
- Canvas: 610×1086, 30 fps, approximately 8 seconds, silent H.264.
- Darkened scene, cream notification card, circular avatar, navy typography, beige accents.
- Launch copy communicates availability; ending-soon copy communicates the three-day deadline.
- Dates, price, campaign name, scenes, avatar, CTA, and output names are dynamic.

The current campaign build entry point is `scripts/build-campaign-reels.sh`.
