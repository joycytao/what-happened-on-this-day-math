# Product Reel

Use `scripts/generate-product-reel.mjs` for the workstream and `scripts/build-product-reel.mjs` for the template/render handoff. Read `reels/product-reel-template/DESIGN.md` first.

Require a verified monthly release, month-specific product name and URLs, and real cover, preview, reading-passage, Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 assets. Generate four distinct weekly hooks. Keep the product evidence primary, avoid unsupported claims, do not expose answer keys, and create the paired Carousel input contract.
