# Perspective Carousel

Use the template at `social/perspective-carousel-template/`. Build a complete, saveable explanation rather than a Reel storyboard. The default structure is: lifestyle hook → context/problem → perspective → practical shift → closing CTA.

Keep copy conversational, evidence-based, and readable on a 1080×1350 canvas. Use real supplied visuals when evidence is needed. Do not expose personal data or make unsupported claims about a child, grade level, mastery, or outcomes. Produce the slide set, caption, and hashtags.
