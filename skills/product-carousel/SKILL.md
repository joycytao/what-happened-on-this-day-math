# Product Carousel

Use `scripts/generate-product-carousel.mjs` with the template at `social/product-carousel-template/`. The current workstream creates four weekly Carousel records paired to four Product Reel records, normally 5–8 slides each.

Require verified cover, preview, reading-passage, Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 assets. Keep the same month and redirect URL as the paired Product Reel. Do not expose answer keys or invent grade levels, outcomes, testimonials, or product claims.
