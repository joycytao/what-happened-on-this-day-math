# Carousel Content Plan

## Purpose

Create four Carousels that extend the four weekly Product Reels for one monthly
package. Each Carousel has the same core idea as its week's Product Reel, but
uses a more explanatory first slide and a saveable structure.

The Carousel's job is to help an interested viewer:

- understand the product in more detail;
- inspect the relevant evidence;
- save the post for later review;
- move to the same monthly product link.

## Reusable monthly campaign contract

For each month, prepare:

- 4 Carousels;
- 1 Carousel paired with each Product Reel;
- 5–8 slides as needed by the topic;
- a fixed brand template;
- a first slide with a complete, saveable title;
- evidence from the same product assets used by the paired Reel;
- a final CTA slide using the same monthly redirect URL as the Reel.

The Carousel and Reel must share the same weekly audience problem or product
idea. They do not need to use identical wording:

```text
Reel: short attention hook
Carousel: complete, saveable explanation of that hook
```

## October campaign inputs

- **Product:** October Morning Work
- **Destination:** `6pm-studio.com/go/october`
- **Confirmed product assets:** product cover, product preview, reading
  passage, Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 worksheets
- **Display rule:** show the cover and preview fully when appropriate; show
  worksheet pages primarily through crops, zooms, and collages until the
  public/internal asset rule is confirmed.

## Fixed Carousel template

Use the same visual system for the first campaign version:

- consistent brand colors, typography, spacing, and footer treatment;
- 1:1 or the approved Instagram Carousel format;
- first slide: complete title based on the Product Reel hook;
- middle slides: product evidence and explanation;
- final slide: fixed CTA and monthly redirect link;
- 5–8 slides, depending on how much evidence the hook requires.

Recommended slide logic:

1. **Title:** state the viewer problem or product question clearly.
2. **Context:** explain what the package offers in relation to that question.
3. **Evidence:** show the relevant reading passage, level, preview, or cover.
4. **Interpretation:** explain what the viewer should notice.
5. **Use case:** show how the package could fit into a routine.
6. **Optional comparison:** include only when it adds new information.
7. **Optional recap:** summarize the package value in one sentence.
8. **CTA:** show the product name and monthly URL.

Do not force all eight slides when five communicate the idea clearly.

## Four-week October Carousel campaign

### Week 1 — Package overview

**Paired Product Reel idea:** A simple way to make monthly math practice feel
doable.

**Carousel title:**

> What is inside October Morning Work?

**Suggested slides:**

1. `What is inside October Morning Work?`
2. `A monthly package built around story-based math practice`
3. `Reading passage`
4. `Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 math pages`
5. `Use the preview to see how the package is organized`
6. `Find October Morning Work on TPT`

**Visual brief:** Use the October cover on the first or final slide, the preview
for structure, and cropped worksheet details for the contents. Keep the page
collage readable rather than showing too many tiny pages at once.

**Caption direction:** Give a concise contents summary and invite the viewer to
save the post as a package reference.

**CTA slide:**

> Explore October Morning Work
> `6pm-studio.com/go/october`

### Week 2 — Differentiated levels

**Paired Product Reel idea:** One monthly topic can need different levels of
support.

**Carousel title:**

> How the three levels of October Morning Work are different

**Suggested slides:**

1. `How the three levels are different`
2. `One monthly package, three ways into the math`
3. `Level 1: show the relevant task crop`
4. `Level 2: show the relevant task crop`
5. `Level 3: show the relevant task crop`
6. `Compare the support, wording, or task structure visible in the preview`
7. `Choose the page that fits the learner and context`
8. `Explore October Morning Work on TPT`

**Visual brief:** Use a consistent crop size for all three levels. Add labels
that identify the level, but do not claim a universal grade or ability match
unless the product documentation explicitly confirms it.

**Caption direction:** Explain that the package provides differentiated entry
points. Encourage viewers to inspect the preview for the actual differences.

**CTA slide:**

> See all three levels in October Morning Work
> `6pm-studio.com/go/october`

### Week 3 — Reading and math connection

**Paired Product Reel idea:** One monthly topic can connect reading and math.

**Carousel title:**

> How October Morning Work connects a reading passage to math practice

**Suggested slides:**

1. `How reading and math connect in one monthly package`
2. `Start with the reading passage`
3. `Notice the details and numbers in the passage`
4. `Move into the related math practice`
5. `Compare the three level options shown in the preview`
6. `A connected routine can make the materials easier to navigate`
7. `Explore the full October package`

**Visual brief:** Use a reading-passage crop first, then a controlled transition
to math-page crops. Only show relationships that are visible in the actual
October materials.

**Caption direction:** Describe the reading-to-math sequence without claiming
that the Carousel displays every daily page.

**CTA slide:**

> Explore October Morning Work
> `6pm-studio.com/go/october`

### Week 4 — Routine and use case

**Paired Product Reel idea:** A ready-to-use monthly math routine.

**Carousel title:**

> A simple way to use October Morning Work throughout the month

**Suggested slides:**

1. `A simple way to use October Morning Work throughout the month`
2. `Choose the day's reading passage`
3. `Select the math level that fits the learner and context`
4. `Use the page as morning work, homeschool practice, or extra practice when appropriate`
5. `Repeat the routine with the next available page`
6. `Keep the cover and preview nearby when planning`
7. `Find the full package on TPT`

**Visual brief:** Use the cover, preview, and a small number of worksheet crops.
Do not fabricate a classroom or family photograph. The Carousel can explain a
possible use case without presenting it as a real testimonial.

**Caption direction:** Present the routine as an example rather than a required
method. Invite viewers to choose the use case that fits their setting.

**CTA slide:**

> Find October Morning Work on TPT
> `6pm-studio.com/go/october`

## Asset handling and review gates

Before publishing any Carousel:

- Confirm that every shown asset belongs to the month's package.
- Use the cover and preview as complete product-identification assets.
- Use worksheet crops, zooms, and collages while the full-page display policy is
  still unconfirmed.
- Do not show the answer key unless its public-display status is explicitly
  confirmed.
- Make sure the first slide's title matches the paired Reel's core idea.
- Make sure the final slide uses the same monthly redirect URL as the paired
  Reel.
- Check that no slide makes an unsupported claim about grade level, mastery,
  classroom use, or family results.

## Measurement plan

Track each Carousel as part of the same weekly Reel pair:

- **Saves:** whether the explanation is useful for later;
- **Shares:** whether the information is relevant to another teacher or parent;
- **Profile visits:** whether the product explanation creates further interest;
- **Link clicks:** whether the CTA moves the viewer toward the monthly product;
- **Carousel completion signals:** use available platform data when available.

Keep the fixed visual system for the first four posts so the campaign tests the
weekly topic and hook rather than changing every variable at once. Consider
more flexible layouts only after the first campaign produces comparison data.

## Monthly replacement checklist

When starting a new month:

- replace the month name, product name, and redirect URL;
- verify the available cover, preview, reading passage, and level assets;
- pair each Carousel with the correct week's Product Reel hook;
- rewrite the title and slide copy for the new package;
- remove any slide whose evidence is not available for that month;
- confirm the public-display rule before using full-page imagery;
- verify that every final CTA points to the new month's redirect URL.
