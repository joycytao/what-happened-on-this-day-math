# TPT Positioning Brief — 6PM Studio Morning Work Math

**Status:** Approved reference copy strategy, version 1

This document is the source of truth for future landing-page and TPT listing
copy. Product metadata still follows `docs/tpt-metadata-taxonomy.md`; this
brief supplies positioning, audience language, claims boundaries, and approved
copy direction that the metadata generator should reference.

## Product

**Brand:** 6PM Studio  
**Reference product:** October Morning Work Math Worksheets | Daily Math Word Problems | Historical Mini-Stories | 3 Levels

## Core promise

One historical mini-story. One focused math word problem. Three levels of
challenge—ready to print and use for daily practice.

## Target buyers

- Elementary classroom teachers
- Homeschool parents with multiple children

## Buyer problems

1. They need no-prep math practice that can be used immediately each day.
2. Students in the same classroom or family need different levels of challenge.
3. Traditional math practice often lacks a memorable context.

## Product evidence

- One daily math word problem for every day in the month.
- Three levels are included in the same product.
- Level 1: K–2
- Level 2: Grades 1–3
- Level 3: Grades 2–5
- Each level has its own answer key.
- US Letter portrait format.
- The three levels share the same daily historical mini-story.
- No-prep, print-and-use PDF format.

## Claims boundaries

Do not claim a fixed mathematics skill progression. Do not position the
product as a complete reading-comprehension curriculum. Historical stories
provide memorable context for the math routine; they are not a separate
reading curriculum.

- **One daily routine:** A predictable format for morning work, bell ringers,
  independent practice, homework, or early finishers.

## Approved FAQ language

### What is included?

October includes 31 days of math word problems, three levels of difficulty,
shared historical mini-stories, and a separate answer key for each level.

### How are the three math levels different?

Each level uses the same historical context while adjusting the number range
and reasoning steps.

### Can students use the same story?

Yes. Students can begin with the same daily historical mini-story and then
complete the level that fits their current needs.

### Are answer keys included?

Yes. Each level includes its own answer key for easy teacher or parent
checking.
