# Product Reels Content Plan

## Purpose

Create four Product Reels for one monthly learning package. The four Reels are
published across four weeks, use different hooks, and continue to promote the
same package and monthly destination link.

The Product Reel's job is product understanding:

> Help a new viewer understand what the monthly package is, what it contains,
> and when it could fit into a family's or teacher's routine.

This is separate from the Utility Reel, whose job is to introduce a practical
review workflow.

## Reusable monthly campaign contract

For each month, prepare:

- 4 Product Reels;
- 4 distinct hooks adapted from the approved hook framework list;
- 1 fixed visual template used across the first campaign version;
- 1 corresponding Carousel for each Product Reel;
- 1 monthly redirect link used by all four Reels;
- product evidence from the month's cover, preview, reading passage, and
  differentiated levels when available.

Replace these fields for each month:

```text
[MONTH_NAME]
[PRODUCT_NAME]
[MONTHLY_REDIRECT_URL]
[PACKAGE_FEATURES]
[USE_CASE]
[AVAILABLE_PRODUCT_ASSETS]
```

## October campaign inputs

Use these October inputs in the first example campaign:

- **Product:** October Morning Work
- **Destination:** `6pm-studio.com/go/october`
- **Confirmed product assets:** product cover, product preview, reading
  passage, Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 worksheets
- **Display rule:** show the cover and preview fully when appropriate; show
  worksheet pages primarily through crops, zooms, and collages until the
  public/internal asset rule is confirmed.

## Fixed Reel production format

The first campaign version uses one repeatable visual template:

- 9:16 vertical;
- no voiceover;
- English on-screen text;
- real product visuals as the primary evidence;
- music or ambient audio;
- flexible length based on the amount of product evidence needed;
- final 2–3 seconds reserved for the monthly CTA.

The fixed template should use this sequence:

1. **Hook** — one short audience-facing sentence.
2. **Product evidence** — show the relevant package asset.
3. **Meaning** — explain what the viewer is seeing or why it matters.
4. **Package identity** — show the product name or cover.
5. **CTA** — show the monthly redirect URL.

The exact duration may change, but every Reel must include at least one clear
product-evidence shot. Do not replace the product evidence with abstract brand
footage unless a week's confirmed product assets are insufficient.

## Hook rules

The first campaign version adapts the ten approved hook frameworks supplied for
6PM Studio. Hooks should be rewritten to fit the actual monthly package; do not
paste a generic template unchanged.

Each week's hook must differ in wording and entry point. Across the four weeks,
rotate among:

- an audience problem;
- a package feature;
- a use case or learning moment.

The hook may be emotionally framed, but the following product shot must provide
visible evidence for the claim. Avoid promising a result that the package itself
does not establish.

## Four-week October campaign

### Week 1 — Package overview

**Hook framework:** `I think I just discovered the best...`

**Product Reel hook:**

> I think I just discovered a simple way to make monthly math practice feel doable.

**Overlay copy:**

1. `A full month of story-based math practice`
2. `Reading passage + Level 1 + Level 2 + Level 3`
3. `October Morning Work`
4. `See the full package on TPT`

**Shot brief:**

1. Open with the October product cover.
2. Move through the product preview.
3. Show a reading-passage crop.
4. Show a collage of Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 crops.
5. End on the cover and CTA.

**Caption direction:** Explain that this Reel is a quick package tour. Keep the
caption focused on what is included rather than making a broad learning claim.

**CTA:** `Find October Morning Work: 6pm-studio.com/go/october`

### Week 2 — Differentiated levels

**Hook framework:** `What no one tells you about...`

**Product Reel hook:**

> What no one tells you about math practice: one topic can need different levels of support.

**Overlay copy:**

1. `Same monthly theme`
2. `Three levels of math practice`
3. `Choose the page that fits the learner`
4. `Explore October Morning Work`

**Shot brief:**

1. Start with three cropped worksheet details arranged side by side.
2. Label them `Level 1`, `Level 2`, and `Level 3`.
3. Briefly zoom into the different task structures.
4. Return to the October cover.
5. End on the CTA.

**Caption direction:** Describe the three-level structure without claiming that
one level is universally correct for a child. Invite viewers to inspect the
preview for the level differences.

**CTA:** `Find October Morning Work: 6pm-studio.com/go/october`

### Week 3 — Reading and math connection

**Hook framework:** `I used to believe... until I realized...`

**Product Reel hook:**

> I used to believe reading and math had to be practiced separately—until I saw how one monthly topic can connect them.

**Overlay copy:**

1. `Start with the reading passage`
2. `Notice the numbers and details`
3. `Move into differentiated math practice`
4. `October Morning Work`

**Shot brief:**

1. Show a reading-passage crop.
2. Use a controlled zoom on a relevant detail or number.
3. Transition from the passage to a math-page crop.
4. Show the three level labels briefly.
5. End with the cover and CTA.

**Caption direction:** Explain the sequence from reading passage to math task.
Do not imply that the Reel shows every page in the package.

**CTA:** `Find October Morning Work: 6pm-studio.com/go/october`

### Week 4 — Routine and use case

**Hook framework:** `If you're... this is for you.`

**Product Reel hook:**

> If you are looking for a ready-to-use monthly math routine, this is for you.

**Overlay copy:**

1. `Choose a day`
2. `Read the short passage`
3. `Complete the math page that fits`
4. `Repeat throughout the month`
5. `Explore October Morning Work`

**Shot brief:**

1. Show the cover or preview as the starting point.
2. Show a reading-passage crop.
3. Show one differentiated math crop.
4. Show a small collage suggesting repeated daily use without inventing a
   classroom or family scene.
5. End with the CTA.

**Caption direction:** Position the product as a ready-to-use resource for a
   routine. Avoid claiming that every family or classroom will use it in the
   same way.

**CTA:** `Find October Morning Work: 6pm-studio.com/go/october`

## Asset handling and review gates

Before editing or publishing:

- Confirm that the asset belongs to the month's package.
- Use the cover and preview as complete product-identification assets.
- Use worksheet crops, zooms, or collages while the full-page display policy is
  still unconfirmed.
- Do not show the answer key unless its public-display status is explicitly
  confirmed.
- Check every overlay against the actual page shown on screen.
- Ensure the CTA names the same month as the displayed product.
- Check that the Reel communicates the package without implying an unverified
  classroom, parent, or student testimonial.

## Metrics

Track each Reel separately while evaluating the four-week campaign as one unit:

- **Reach:** discovery;
- **Profile visits:** interest in the brand;
- **Saves and shares:** usefulness or clarity;
- **Link clicks:** product-page intent;
- **TPT product views and sales:** downstream conversion when available.

Do not change the visual template after one post. Use the first campaign to
compare hooks while holding the production format stable. Consider introducing
the later flexible-template approach only after the first four Reels provide
enough evidence to justify the change.

## Monthly replacement checklist

When starting a new month:

- replace `[MONTH_NAME]`, `[PRODUCT_NAME]`, and `[MONTHLY_REDIRECT_URL]`;
- verify the actual available product assets;
- write four new hooks rather than reusing the October wording verbatim;
- map each hook to one product-evidence shot;
- create the matching Carousel using the same weekly core idea;
- confirm public-display status before using a full page;
- check that every CTA points to the new month's redirect URL.
